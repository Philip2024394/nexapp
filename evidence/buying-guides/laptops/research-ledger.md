## Methodology notes

- **Workload mapping** (writing / email, student, developer, creative, light gaming, heavy gaming, video editing) is derived primarily from L1, L3, L4, L5. Where sources diverge on a "sweet spot" we describe the range, not a single number.
- **Spec translation** (CPU tiers, RAM min/sweet/overkill, SSD capacity vs speed, display resolution and panel type, keyboard, ports, battery, thermals) draws on L1–L7, with Intel/AMD/Apple official pages (L11–L13) used only to frame tier naming conventions — no SKU names appear in the guide.
- **Longevity and repairability** lean on iFixit (L8, L9, L25) and Framework (L10) as the published benchmark; Microsoft lifecycle (L16) and ChromeOS AUE (L20) establish software-support horizons.
- **Used / refurbished checks** combine L25 (battery cycles), L14 (Apple activation lock), L15 (Windows licence state), L20 (ChromeOS AUE), L23 (UK consumer refurb grading observations), and L21–L22 (observed buyer questions on keyboard wear / screen hours / hinge condition).
- **Where a claim could not be independently confirmed** across at least two of the above source tiers, it is omitted rather than hedged.
