**Scope:** Evergreen editorial research supporting a how-to-judge-quality guide for women's and unisex handbags across leather, synthetic, bonded and fabric constructions. Independent of brand. No affiliate relationships.

## Source inclusion rules

- Prefer primary-source and trade-industry references (tanneries, leather working groups, hardware manufacturers, textile science bodies, consumer desks with published methodology).
- Secondary sources accepted only from recognised reporting outlets (BBC, Reuters, Vogue Business, Business of Fashion) or long-running artisan trade blogs with disclosed authorship.
- Rejected: affiliate "best bag" listicles, anonymous SEO content mills, UGC star-rating aggregators, Pinterest-tier how-to pages.

## Notes on usage

- Reddit (S25) is used **only** as a signal of which questions real buyers ask (surfaced in `questions.md` with `[Observed on Google / Reddit]` tags). No assertions in `draft-content.md` rely on it.
- Where ISO or ASTM standards are cited, the guide references the existence and purpose of the test, not paywalled text.
- Manufacturer transparency pages (S23) are cross-checked — not rewritten — and never used to endorse a brand.
- Leather Working Group (S1) ratings describe *tannery* environmental performance, not finished bag quality. The guide makes that distinction explicit so readers don't read "Gold-rated leather" as a quality claim.

## Known gaps

- No independent lab-tested comparative data for synthetic "vegan" leathers from different suppliers; Textile Exchange covers market share, not head-to-head durability. The guide handles this by describing categories (PU, PVC, bio-based, recycled) rather than ranking them.
- Hardware plating longevity data is manufacturer-published; no public independent benchmark exists for plated-vs-solid brass under handbag use. The guide stays qualitative here.
- Counterfeit-detection evidence is deliberately kept generic (misalignment, hardware weight, lining discrepancies as a *concept*) per the brief — there is no brand-specific authentication content.
