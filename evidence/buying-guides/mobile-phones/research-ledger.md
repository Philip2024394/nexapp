**Topic:** How to choose a mobile phone (new, refurbished, used)
**Route:** /nex-generated/buying-guides/mobile-phones
**Compiled:** 2026-11-17
**Compiler:** Nex editorial research (Batch 1, sibling 5)

This ledger records every third-party source consulted while drafting the mobile phones buying guide. Sources are mixed by authority type: consumer-advocacy publications, independent testing labs, repair-industry references, manufacturer policy pages, regulators and standards bodies, and mainstream news desks. Marketplace-operator pages (Swappa, Back Market, etc.) are used only for operational mechanics (e.g. what an IMEI check returns), never for editorial conclusions. UGC review sites, SEO listicles and affiliate content mills were excluded.

## Authoritative gaps noted

- **RTings smartphone methodology pages** are less publicly linkable than their monitor methodology; RTings' smartphone test matrix exists but the public methodology write-up is thinner than for displays and audio. Claims about "phone display testing" in the guide are kept to the general principle (independent measurement beats spec-sheet claims) rather than specific RTings-derived numbers.
- **UK-specific regulation.** The EU Ecodesign regulation (S10) is in force in the EU. UK equivalents (post-Brexit) are evolving; the Product Security and Telecommunications Infrastructure (PSTI) Act 2022 covers security updates disclosure but the minimum-years figure is not yet set in UK statute the way five years is set in EU statute. The guide treats EU as the citable floor and flags UK as "disclosure required, minimum not legislated to the same depth."
- **"Years of support" by brand** — figures (7 years Google/Samsung flagship, 5-year EU minimum, Apple's practical 6–8 years) change as manufacturers update commitments. The guide anchors the claim to each manufacturer's own policy page (S7, S8, S5) rather than summarising an aggregated number that will drift.
- **Refurbished grading** is not a legally defined scheme — grades A / B / C vary by seller. S1 is cited for the broad pattern; readers are directed to the specific seller's grade definition.

## Excluded sources (and why)

- AI-generated or SEO-flooded sites returned in search results (phonerevolutionhub.com, smartphones.gadgethacks.com "2026 decision framework", theinspectaspect.com, etc.) — excluded; no byline, no editorial accountability, visible templated structure.
- Affiliate-driven "best phones of 2026" listicles — out of scope for a decision-framework guide and would conflict with the no-model-recommendations constraint.
- Social-media UGC threads (Reddit, Macrumors forums, Apple discussions) — read to discover observed buyer questions, but not cited as authority for claims.
