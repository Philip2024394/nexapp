## Preference and exclusion notes

- All UGC sources (S21, S22) are used only to source **observed buyer questions** — never to source factual claims. Claims are backed by standards, academic, trade-education or regulator sources.
- No affiliate listicles, no SEO content mills, no "best wallet" roundups were used.
- Horween tech sheets (S11) are cited only to describe *named* leathers a seller may truthfully reference; they are not used to endorse any product.
- RFID discussion leans on NIST (S14), EMVCo (S15) and peer-reviewed cryptography literature (S17) rather than retailer marketing claims.
- Where a source is a broad hub (e.g. Carryology, r/leathercraft), the specific article or thread used is noted in `questions.md` and `draft-content.md` inline.
